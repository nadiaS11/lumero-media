## Brief

Northwind came to us with 22 hours of rushes from a six-day shoot across northern Iceland and a four-minute target runtime. The brief was simple in stating and difficult in execution: a brand film that *felt* like the company without ever saying the company's name on screen.

## Approach

We assembled chronologically across nine days of edit, then restructured against a three-act story spine — arrival, work, return. The grade leans cool in the open and warms toward the close to mirror the emotional arc; we built a Resolve LUT against ARRI Reveal that the production team can reuse on future shoots.

Sound design was the unlock. The wind, in particular, is doing more narrative work than the voiceover. We layered three location wind tracks across the cut and ducked the music to let it breathe.

## Result

The film carried the brand's market launch and continues to anchor their site. The Resolve project files were handed back to Northwind for archival, with a written grade reference.
