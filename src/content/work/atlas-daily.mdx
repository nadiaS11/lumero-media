## Brief

Atlas Daily is a history-and-civilization channel competing against a saturated niche dominated by template-driven faceless content. Their script quality was strong; the edits were flat. They needed a visual language that read as crafted without sacrificing the four-video-per-week publishing pace.

## Approach

We built a per-channel style guide — typography system, lower-third treatments, B-roll selection rules, transition vocabulary — and trained a small editor team against it. Two editors run the channel on rotation, with one senior pass per video before delivery.

B-roll is hand-sourced across Artgrid, archival public-domain footage, and a small amount of AI-generated material for historical reconstruction shots where licensed footage doesn't exist. Every motion graphic is built in After Effects against the channel template, not pulled from a preset pack.

## Result

Retention curves smoothed within the first month. The channel's identity is now distinct enough that viewers recognize an Atlas Daily edit before the channel logo appears. Network expansion to a sister channel is in development.
