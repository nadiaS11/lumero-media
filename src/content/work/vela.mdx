## Brief

A vertical music-video short for an independent artist's lead single. The brief was a 58-second cut for Reels and TikTok that worked as both a release teaser and a standalone piece of art.

## Approach

We cut to the beat of the hook in the first three seconds and broke the beat at the midpoint to keep the eye honest. The grade leans into the LP's cover-art palette — desaturated teal and a single warm push on skin tones.

## Result

The short outperformed the artist's previous lead-single rollout by an order of magnitude on first-week reach.
